# Buck_Converter_LTspice
Open-loop 12 V to 5 V buck converter simulated in LTspice.

## Specifications
- Input voltage: 12 V
- Target output voltage: 5 V
- Switching frequency: 100 kHz
- Duty cycle: approximately 41.7%
- Inductor: 100 uH
- Capacitor: 22 uF
- Load: 5 ohms
- Diode: 1N5819 Schottky diode
